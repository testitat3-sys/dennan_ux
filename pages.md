To build a comprehensive e-commerce experience for this demographic, the site structure needs to balance high-intent shopping with educational discovery. 

Below are the page categories and specific views to consider for your roadmap.

---

### 1. The Discovery & "Stage" Engine
Since your homepage focuses on stages, these landing pages act as the primary filters for the entire catalog.

* **Stage Landing Pages (Newborn, Expectant, Toddler):** These are essentially "mini-homepages." Instead of just a list of products, they should feature stage-specific curated content, top brands for that age, and relevant "Must-Haves" (e.g., Hospital Bag essentials for the Expectant stage).
* **The Brand Directory:** A dedicated page for all brands. Since your nav expands into brands, this page serves as an SEO-friendly hub where users can filter brands by category (e.g., "Eco-friendly," "Budget," "Premium").
* **Curated Collections (The "Edit"):** Pages like "The Sleep Sanctuary" or "First Steps" that group products across different categories to solve a specific problem.

### 2. The Core E-Commerce Loop
These are the functional pages that drive conversion.

* **Product Listing Page (PLP):** The grid view. Crucial features here include "Quick Add to Cart" and a "Stage Filter" sidebar so users can narrow down results instantly.
* **Product Detail Page (PDP):** * **The Delivery Timer:** A prominent UI element near the "Add to Cart" button.
    * **Age Appropriateness Scale:** A visual indicator confirming if the product fits the user's specific stage.
    * **Parent Reviews:** Including the age of the reviewer’s child to provide context.
* **The Dynamic Cart:** A slide-out or full-page cart that displays the "Delivery by" timer to reinforce the arrival date before the user even starts the checkout.

### 3. Personalization & Account Pages
This is where the "curated products" logic from your homepage is managed.

* **Onboarding / Profile Setup:** A brief, visual flow where users input the child's birth date or the mother's due date. This data powers the homepage curation.
* **User Dashboard:** A "Growing with You" section. It could show the current stage, upcoming milestones, and products relevant to the *next* stage.
* **The Registry / Wishlist:** A vital tool for expectant parents. This page allows users to share lists with family and friends for baby showers.

### 4. Transactional & Post-Purchase
This is where your client’s specific delivery feature takes center stage.

* **Multi-Step Checkout:** * **Shipping Selection:** Where the timer adjusts in real-time based on the chosen carrier speed.
    * **Confirmation Page:** A final "Success" page that reiterates the countdown to arrival.
* **Order Tracking (Live):** A page featuring a visual progress bar (e.g., Processing -> Dispatched -> Out for Delivery) synced with the delivery timer.

---

### Page Architecture Summary

| Category | Primary Pages | Key Features |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Discovery** | Stage Landing, Brand Hub, Editorial Guides | Stage-specific filtering, SEO-rich content. |
| **Shopping** | PLP, PDP, Search Results | Delivery timer, "Age-fit" badges, Quick-buy. |
| **User Data** | Onboarding, Profile, Registry | Due date tracking, Milestone recommendations. |
| **Utility** | Cart, Checkout, Order Tracking | Real-time logistics countdown, Guest checkout. |

### Optional "Value-Add" Pages
To increase retention, you might consider:
* **The Resource Center:** Expert-led articles on sleep training, nutrition, or prenatal health.
* **"Find the Right Fit" Guide:** Specifically for maternity wear or toddler shoes, which have high return rates.
