This file contains the statistical breakdown of all brands extracted from the product files (`convex_products.json` and `close_matches_with_details.json`).

These statistics are used to dynamically sort the **Most Loved Brands** section on the homepage, prioritizing:
1. **Highest Maximum Discount** (descending)
2. **Highest Product Count** (descending)
3. **Alphabetical Name** (ascending)
