Industry Research & Benchmarks

Insights distilled from top-performing sales operations (Salesforce, Kixie, InsideSales)

1. The 5-Minute Speed-to-Lead Benchmark

Research demonstrates that buyer intent degrades exponentially with time. Reaching an inbound customer within 5 minutes results in a 21x qualification rate boost over 30-minute delays.

2. Tiered Intent SLAs

Blanket SLAs hurt productivity. Dennan categorizes leads by intent intensity: Tier 1 (Store Sourcing - 15m), Tier 2 (Wishlist Alerts - 2h), and Tier 3 (Pre-launch signups - 24h).

Convex Database Architecture Proposal

Schema definitions to support native performance tracking in Convex backend

// convex/schema.ts extension for Lead Performance Tracking export const leadPerformanceSchema = { assignedStaffId: v.optional(v.id("users")), assignedAt: v.optional(v.number()), firstContactAt: v.optional(v.number()), performanceScore: v.optional(v.number()), // Calculated 0-100 LHPS score ghostResolutionFlag: v.optional(v.boolean()), slaTier: v.optional(v.union(v.literal("tier1"), v.literal("tier2"), v.literal("tier3"))), }; // Audit & Activity Log Table export const leadActivities = defineTable({ leadId: v.string(), staffId: v.id("users"), type: v.union(v.literal("call"), v.literal("whatsapp"), v.literal("note")), content: v.string(), characterCount: v.number(), createdAt: v.number(), }).index("by_lead", ["leadId"]);
